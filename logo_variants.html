DESIGN REFINEMENT #2

opto by modulus

Dialing in the 't' proportion & scale (monospace width & baseline anchoring) and knocking down the orange electricity.

1. Orange Calibration: De-Electrifying the Hue

SATURATION & LUMINANCE TUNING
#f15a26
Original (High Voltage)
89% Saturation. High contrast, can feel like neon/glow on OLED/dark themes.
#e55722
Warm Ember (Recommended)
Knocks down peak neon vibration by ~12% while keeping full brand energy.
#d6511d
Grounded Terracotta
Richer, deeper brick-orange. Very calm, zero glare, high enterprise feel.
#c44d1b
Deep Bronze-Orange
Warm autumn copper. Subtle accent that lets the blue remain the primary focal point.

The original #f15a26 was the standard saturated print-conversion orange. On deep dark backgrounds (like #0B0F19), it emits an almost fluorescent, electric glare. Shifting to #e55722 or #d6511d removes the harshness while preserving the warm, energetic pairing with Brand Blue (#008ad0).

2. Scaling the 't' to Full Monospace Proportion

EQUAL CELL WIDTH & BASELINE ALIGNMENT
T-1: Curved Hook 't' (Anchored at Baseline 55, Full Width)
Vertical stem sweeps into a right hook anchored at baseline y=55 (flush with bottom of o & p). Crossbar spans full monospace width at x-height y=29.
T-2: Straight Stem 't' (Anchored at 55, Full Monospace Crossbar)
Straight architectural cross: stem terminates exactly at baseline y=55. Crossbar spans 34px width at x-height y=29.
T-3: Architectural 't' with Baseline Foot (Anchored at 55)
Has a subtle horizontal base at y=55 so the letter cell has identical width top and bottom.
Light Mode: T-1 (Curved Hook 't' with Calibrated Orange #e55722)
Light Mode: T-2 (Straight Stem 't' with Calibrated Orange #e55722)

Why these changes fix the scale:
1. Baseline Anchor (y=55): In the previous draft, the crossbar was at y=33 (below the top of the 'o's at y=29), making the whole character feel slumped and diminutive. Raising the crossbar to y=29 aligns it with the exact x-height of the wordmark.
2. Equal Width: The crossbar width has been scaled from 24px up to 30–34px, matching the full cell width of the circular letters (o and p).
3. The Hook (T-1): Adding the curve at the bottom (using the same r=12 curvature as the u in Modulus) anchors the letter firmly at the baseline and gives it the signature organic flow of geometric sans fonts.
